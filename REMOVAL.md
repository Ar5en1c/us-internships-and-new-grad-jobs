# Removing or fixing a row

If a role is closed, wrong, or you want your company's roles taken off the list, open an issue using the "Wrong or closed row" template. Paste the link from the row.

You can also email founder@youxai.app with the link. We reply within 2 business days and remove or fix the row. Removed rows go into `data/blocklist.json`, so they do not come back on the next update.

The list holds only a title, an employer name, a location, a date and a link to the employer's own posting. It holds no job description text.
